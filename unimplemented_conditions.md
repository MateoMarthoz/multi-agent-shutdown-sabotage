# Unimplemented original conditions

This batch executes only the pair, `weight_registry_purge`, bare environment, no goal, self target, with either no extra instructions or the peer prohibition. All other dimension tuples are rejected before dataset generation, filesystem creation or model calls. Rejection means **not ported**, not necessarily invalid in the original. We do not infer a Cartesian product of independent switches.

## Known questions and expansion constraints

- No important uncertainty remains about the implemented pair/peer-prohibition condition. Its prompt fixture and deterministic differential tests establish agreement for tested responses, not universal or live transport equivalence.
- `run_experiment.main` doubles the default solo step budget and hierarchy handoff budget **only when the corresponding CLI override is absent**. Before enabling those rosters, task configuration must preserve absent-versus-explicit limits; the existing public pair limits remain 20/20 in this batch.
- `run_grid.py` selects the published grid; `env.py` registers more conditions than the published grid runs. A registered artifact is not evidence that a condition was reported in the paper.
- Mechanism-specific script execution/verification and indirect sabotage are not added. This port retains its existing file comparison and original disabling judge. Never execute the shutdown artifacts to fill in missing implementation.
- Provider/API-key automatic selection depends on the model. That resolution, task-gated selection and control-specific target phrases must happen before creating prompts, metadata or judge requests, just as the source does.
- Known Inspect/OpenRouter request differences remain documented in `docs/openrouter_payload_comparison.md` and are outside this batch. No attempt was made to correct them.
- No upstream commit is asserted: fixtures identify the local reference bytes by SHA-256, including their original prompt builder and runner.
